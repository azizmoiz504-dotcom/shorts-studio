Science notes for the pictures: bees see UV, blue and green (no red); sunflower petals have UV-absorbing bases and
UV-reflecting tips, so in bee colours the middle looks dark and the tips bright. Reindeer: snow reflects UV strongly,
lichen and fur absorb it and look dark.
